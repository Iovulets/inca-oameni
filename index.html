About

In a world where it’s hard to keep up with technology, one thing stays the same — we are people in a world of people.

An event by Transylvania Spoken Word, bringing Maria Ungureanu, Tina Suciu, Paul Cristian and Cătălin Iovu to the stage.

Discover 4 different perspectives in a spoken word poetry show that takes you back to an analog world — among friends and beautiful people, with emotions on your face and vibrations in your heart.

After the show, stay for the social night. We’ll play jazz and UK garage, so come ready to mingle and dance.
